    - Deep TypeScript on the server, not just in components — you know where the type system earns its keep

    - Strong Postgres: schema design, query performance, migrations

    - Solid React and TypeScript on the frontend — enough to build and ship a complete feature without a frontend engineer alongside you

    - API design experience (REST, gRPC, or GraphQL) and an opinion on when to reach for which

    - Comfortable owning your own deployments — Docker, CI/CD, cloud environments (AWS or GCP)

    - A track record of shipping without a detailed spec and without process handed to you

    - Comfort working in the US timezone (overlapping working hours required)
