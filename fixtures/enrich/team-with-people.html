Our process
TRIARE covers a full cycle of software development starting with the Discovery phase and ending with the release and maintenance. It's possible with a help of using worldwide methodologies such as Agile, Scrum, Kanban, CI/CD, Gitflow, etc.
01
Discovery
At this stage, we dive into the business processes and gather the information that will be the basis for the next steps. As an outcome we provide: Business case description, User definition and flow diagrams, Architecture documentation.
02
Design
We analyse product requirements and prepare a vivid concept. We design and create stunning UI/UX according to popular patterns and iOS/Android style guides.
03
Development
At this stage, all the pieces come together under a well-managed mobile or web development. We work with the Scrum methodology that allows having a team that is focused and collaborative and doesn’t require babysitting.
04
Testing
Our Quality Assurance team catches any bug before deploying the product and lets us focus on high quality during each development cycle.
05
Release and warranty
We always provide you with a warranty period. Simply put, we provide a warranty because we are truly confident in our work.
06
Support
Finally, we remain a technical partner for as long as the client needs us. TRIARE introduces cost savings, stable infrastructure, and reliable DevOps to secure one’s business operations.

FAQ
What services does TRIARE provide in software development?

Within the software development services, our team supports your project with a full-fledged range of solutions. TRIARE experts start with a deep discovery phase, where they analyze your business needs, its specifics, and current trends to shape the final product. At this stage, we also design the system architecture, define its logic, and select the technologies for its development.

Then, we step into the design stage that also includes extra steps, like UX research, creating mockups, and prototypes. If needed, our design team can also handle branding creation. Once everything is approved, we then move on to the development stage itself.

Following Agile methodologies and using the latest tech (including AI), our team creates the product architecture, its interface, server logic, works with API, connects third-party integrations, payment services, CRMs, and realizes other tech tweaks based on your specific requirements. After it’s ready, we carefully test everything and make adjustments if needed.

Then comes the DevOps stage, where our team works on CI/CD setup, servers, and cloud infrastructure. The final step – post-launch support that covers updates, optimization, and post-release product development. We’ll support you during the development and after the product goes live to be sure it works as expected, handles increased user load, and supports your business goals effectively.

How long has TRIARE been working in the software development industry?

We have built effective software solutions worldwide since 2015. To date, we have more than 10 years of experience in creating effective products for businesses that want to power up internal processes and for innovators striving to create brand-new products that bring meaningful value. 

What technologies does the TRIARE team use to build digital products?

To create scalable and result-oriented custom mobile apps, corporate systems, and SaaS platforms, we rely on the following technologies. 

Mobile development: Swift (iOS), Kotlin (Android), React Native & Flutter for cross-platform mobile apps development. 

Web development: React, Angular, Node.js, Python, and PHP. Our experts use the WordPress website builder for designing web platforms that deliver maximum productivity. 

How does TRIARE ensure the quality and reliability of its software solutions?

To make sure the software solution we built is ready to go live and trouble-free, our QA specialists run automated and load testing at every development stage. This way, we ensure the product’s safety, productivity, and readiness to collect first users.
