Our story

The Rubyroid Labs story begins with two Ruby geeks who built a team of like-minded professionals in 2013.

Since then, we have been developing web and mobile applications for startups, established businesses and enterprises. In addition to custom development, we have also helped companies cut tech-related costs and make the most of their IT infrastructure.

Our core values and benefits

  1. We build with proven technical depth

    • End-to-end mobile and web development expertise

      With 13+ years in Rails and 7+ years in React, we handle everything from your database to your mobile app, building solutions that grow with you.

    • Design that works

      We design interfaces that balance user needs with business goals, using research and testing to create experiences that feel natural and convert effectively.

    • AI integration experience

      We help you stay competitive with AI-powered features, from natural language processing to computer vision, integrated thoughtfully into your product.

  2. We're the team you can rely on

    • Rescuing unstable systems

      We stabilize failing codebases and rescue struggling projects, turning technical problems into solid foundations you can build on with confidence.

    • Thorough testing at every stage

      Comprehensive automated and manual testing ensures your code works as expected, reducing bugs and giving you confidence in every release.

    • Responsive maintenance support

      When issues arise, we respond within 1 hour during business hours, keeping your systems running smoothly and your users happy.

  3. We grow by sharing

    • Community contributors

      We give back to Ruby and React ecosystems through open-source contributions that developers around the world rely on.

    • Supporting organizations in need

      Every year we contribute a certain amount to charitable organizations to make lives better and build a future we're proud of.

    • Cross-team learning sessions

      Weekly hackathons where developers, designers, PMs, and marketers show what they've discovered lately and help the team stay current with new technologies and tools.
