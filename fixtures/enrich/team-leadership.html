Dave Cohen

CEO

Dave is the CEO & Co-founder of Utility. Having spent a decade running large-scale technology groups within the sports and media industries, Dave started Utility in 2013 to serve as a partner to both venture and enterprise companies looking to create new digital businesses from the ground up. Having overseen many dozens of end-to-end digital tech products and teams within highly-regarded institutions like Major League Baseball and CBS Sports, Dave aims to continue making Utility a leading agency in the space by bringing together the best and most experienced leadership who partner with clients to bring visions to life. Dave received his MBA from Columbia University, where he currently serves as an Advisor at the Columbia Startup Lab, and received his BS from Lehigh University.

Kevin Friedman

CTO

Kevin is the Chief Technology Officer of Utility. He is a career-long product engineering leader, experienced in building high-performing tech teams and organizations. At Utility, he is responsible for the company's technical strategy, formulation, development, and cross-functional delivery of their award-winning mobile and web applications, as well as recruiting the best technical talent in the industry. In his previous role as CTO at Teachers Pay Teachers, he led engineering efforts for the world's largest marketplace of peer-to-peer educational materials which used by over 7 million educators and had over 1 billion downloads worldwide. Prior to this, as the Engineering Manager at The New York Public Library, Kevin led the digital transformation efforts of mobile and web application teams creating their next-generation research catalog, launching e-reading initiatives, and modernizing the organization’s website serving over 18 million patrons annually. Kevin received his BS from New York University.
